# CSS - Central Superior Services Exam

## Category & Description

The **Central Superior Services (CSS)** examination is Pakistan's most prestigious nationwide competitive examination for recruitment into civil services (BPS-17 and above). It serves as the gateway to 12 elite occupational groups including:

- Pakistan Administrative Service (PAS)
- Police Service of Pakistan
- Foreign Service of Pakistan
- Postal Group
- Military Lands & Cantonments Group
- Information Group
- Commerce & Trade Group
- District Management Group
- Office Management Group
- Customs & Excise Group
- Income Tax Group
- Audit & Accounts Group

Successfully passing the CSS exam guarantees a career in public administration, policy-making, and governance at the federal level.

## Conducting Body

**Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)**  
Government of Pakistan

## Subjects

### Compulsory Subjects (600 marks):
1. **Essay** (100 marks)
2. **English (Precis & Composition)** (100 marks)
3. **General Science & Ability** (100 marks)
4. **Current Affairs** (100 marks)
5. **Pakistan Affairs** (100 marks)
6. **Islamic Studies / Ethics** (100 marks for Muslims / Non-Muslims)

### Optional Subjects (600 marks):
Choose 6 subjects from diverse groups including:
- **Group I:** Accounting & Auditing, Anthropology, Applied Mathematics, Business Administration, Computer Science, Economics, Geography, Journalism, Political Science, Psychology, Public Administration, Statistics
- **Group II:** English Literature, Everyday Science, History of Pakistan & India, International Relations, Islamic History & Culture, Philosophy, Sociology
- **Group III:** Arabic, English (Précis & Composition), French, German, Persian, Urdu

**Total Marks:** 1200 (Compulsory 600 + Optional 600)

## Syllabus Breakdown

### Compulsory Subjects:

#### 1. Essay (100 marks)
- Current socio-economic and political issues
- Philosophical and abstract topics
- Scientific and technological developments
- Environmental and global challenges
- Pakistan's role in international affairs

#### 2. English (Precis & Composition) (100 marks)
- **Précis Writing:** Condensing passages to one-third length
- **Comprehension:** Understanding and analyzing complex texts
- **Grammar & Usage:** Sentence correction, error identification
- **Vocabulary:** Synonyms, antonyms, idioms, phrases
- **Essay Writing:** Argumentative, descriptive, narrative styles

#### 3. General Science & Ability (100 marks)
- **Science (50 marks):** Basic concepts from matric/intermediate level (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- **Ability (50 marks):** 
  - Logical Reasoning (arguments, assumptions, inferences)
  - Analytical Reasoning (patterns, sequences, puzzles)
  - Basic Numeracy (percentages, ratios, time-distance problems)

#### 4. Current Affairs (100 marks)
- Domestic political developments
- Regional geopolitics (South Asia, Middle East)
- International organizations (UN, WTO, IMF, World Bank)
- Global economic trends
- Climate change and sustainability
- Technology and innovation

#### 5. Pakistan Affairs (100 marks)
- Constitutional history of Pakistan
- Major political movements and milestones
- Socio-economic challenges
- Foreign policy and relations
- Governance and administrative structure

#### 6. Islamic Studies / Ethics (100 marks)
- Quranic teachings and Sunnah (for Muslims)
- Islamic jurisprudence and Fiqh
- Ethics, morality, and professional conduct (for non-Muslims)

### Optional Subjects:
- Detailed syllabi available on FPSC website for each optional subject
- Recommended scoring subjects: **Anthropology, Public Administration, Criminology, Everyday Science**

## Test Pattern

### Written Examination:
- **Total Papers:** 12 (6 Compulsory + 6 Optional)
- **Total Marks:** 1200
- **Duration:** 3 hours per paper
- **Passing Criteria:**
  - Minimum 40% in each compulsory paper
  - Minimum 33% in each optional paper
  - Minimum 50% aggregate (600/1200)

### Post-Written Stages:
1. **Medical Test:** Fitness assessment
2. **Psychological Assessment:** Aptitude and personality evaluation
3. **Viva Voce (Interview):** 300 marks

**Final Merit:** Written (1200) + Viva (300) = **1500 marks**

---

## Difficulty Level

**Extremely High**

- **Success Rate:** 2-4% annually (only 200-300 candidates selected from 10,000-15,000 who appear)
- **Main Challenges:**
  - Vast syllabus spanning 12 diverse subjects
  - Subjective paper evaluation (especially Essay and Precis)
  - High competition for limited positions
  - Requires 12-18 months of dedicated preparation
  - Screening heavily depends on English Essay and Precis papers

**Why It's Challenging:**
- Not just knowledge-based; requires analytical thinking, current affairs awareness, and excellent writing skills
- Viva Voce panel includes senior bureaucrats testing personality, general awareness, and decision-making
- Long preparation period tests consistency and mental endurance

## Roadmap

### 30-Day Crash Plan (Last-Minute Revision)

**Target:** Candidates who have already covered syllabus once

**Daily Schedule (8-10 hours):**
- **Morning (3 hours):** Essay writing practice (1 essay daily)
- **Afternoon (3 hours):** Precis practice + English grammar revision
- **Evening (2-3 hours):** Current Affairs compilation (newspapers + monthly magazines)
- **Night (1-2 hours):** Compulsory subjects revision (GSA, Pakistan Affairs, Islamic Studies)

**Weekly Breakdown:**
- **Week 1-2:** Focus on English (Essay + Precis) - write 14 essays
- **Week 3:** Current Affairs intensive - compile last 6 months' events
- **Week 4:** Mock tests for all compulsory papers + final revision

**Key Focus:**
- Master Essay structure (introduction, body, conclusion)
- Précis accuracy (one-third length, no personal opinion)
- Current Affairs indexing (topic-wise notes)

---

### 3-Month Standard Plan (Core Preparation)

**Target:** Candidates with basic knowledge foundation

**Monthly Breakdown:**

#### **Month 1: Compulsory Subjects Foundation**
- **Week 1-2:** 
  - Complete GSA syllabus (Science: revise matric/intermediate concepts)
  - Start Ability section practice (daily 30 MCQs - reasoning + numeracy)
- **Week 3-4:**
  - Complete Pakistan Affairs (constitution, major events, foreign policy)
  - Start Islamic Studies / Ethics syllabus
- **Daily:** Read 2 newspapers (Dawn, The News) + note-taking

#### **Month 2: Optional Subjects Selection + Preparation**
- **Week 1:** Select 2 easiest optional subjects based on background
- **Week 2-4:** Complete syllabus for 2 optional subjects
- **Daily:** Essay writing practice (3 essays per week)
- **Weekly:** One full-length précis practice

#### **Month 3: Current Affairs + Mock Tests**
- **Week 1-2:** Intensive Current Affairs revision (last 12 months)
- **Week 3:** Weekly mock tests for all 8 subjects (6 compulsory + 2 optional)
- **Week 4:** Final revision + time management practice

**Daily Practice:**
- Newspaper reading: 1 hour
- Essay writing: 1 hour (alternate days)
- Subject study: 6-7 hours

---

### 6-Month Comprehensive Plan (Complete Preparation)

**Target:** Serious candidates aiming for top ranks

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Foundation Building**
- **Compulsory Subjects:**
  - Complete all 6 compulsory subjects (syllabus coverage)
  - Daily Essay practice (4 essays per week)
  - Daily Précis practice (2 per week)
  - GSA Ability section: Daily 50 MCQs
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Morning: 2 newspapers + note-taking (2 hours)
  - Afternoon: Subject study (4 hours)
  - Evening: Essay/Précis writing (2 hours)
  - Night: Revision (1-2 hours)

**Phase 2 (Month 3-4): Optional Subjects Mastery**
- Complete all 6 optional subjects (100 marks each)
- Subject-wise notes preparation
- Past papers analysis (last 10 years)
- Monthly mock tests for each optional

**Phase 3 (Month 5): Current Affairs Deep Dive**
- **Week 1-2:** Domestic affairs (political, economic, social issues)
- **Week 3:** Regional affairs (India, Afghanistan, China, Middle East)
- **Week 4:** International affairs (UN, climate change, global economy)
- Compile indexed notes (topic-wise, date-wise)

**Phase 4 (Month 6): Mock Tests + Viva Preparation**
- **Week 1-2:** Full-length mock tests (all 12 papers under exam conditions)
- **Week 3:** Viva Voce preparation
  - Current affairs discussions
  - Mock interviews with seniors
  - SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
- **Week 4:** Final revision + mental preparation

**Recommended Resources:**
- Books: Caravan (Pakistan Affairs), Mutalia Pakistan (Urdu), Advanced Study in Pakistan Affairs
- For Essay: Dawn editorials, The News columns, International magazines (The Economist, Foreign Affairs)
- Optional subjects: Standard textbooks + coaching notes

**Success Tips:**
- Join a CSS academy or study group for peer learning
- Attend monthly Viva Voce mock sessions
- Maintain daily journal for Current Affairs
- Practice handwriting speed (write 3-4 pages in 20 minutes)
